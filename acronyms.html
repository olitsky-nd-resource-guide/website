Acronyms

You will see these on flyers, emails and around campus. Here is what each one stands for and what it actually does.

Short nameWhat it stands forWhat it is

C

CaPS
Counseling and Psychological Services
Free, confidential counseling, groups and skills clinics.
See related help: CaPS
CELD
Community Engagement and Leadership Development
The office that runs the CMU Pantry.
See related help: CELD
CPDC Also in this guide
Career and Professional Development Center
Career consulting, job and internship help, and Handshake.
See related help: CPDC
CUC
Cohon University Center
The student center on campus. The Center and the SIT office are here too.

O

ODR Also in this guide
Office of Disability Resources
Where you request accommodations, like extra exam time.
See related help: ODR

S

SASC Also in this guide
Student Academic Success Center
Success Coaching, study workshops, Supplemental Instruction and tutoring.
See related help: SASC
SESF
Student Emergency Support Funding
Help with unexpected costs, from the Office of the Dean of Students.
SI
Supplemental Instruction
Weekly peer-led study sessions for courses many students find hard.
See related help: SI
SIO
Student Information Online
Where you log in to review your financial aid.
SIT
Student Involvement and Traditions
Campus events and student organizations.
See related help: SIT

T

TESF
Tartan Emergency Support Fund
Grant funding for short-term basic needs. You don’t repay it.
See related help: TESF

U

UHS
University Health Services
Medical care by appointment, including sick visits and prescription refills.
See related help: UHS

Based on the Neurodiversity Resource Showcase acronym guide. Entries marked “Also in this guide” were added because they appear on our resource pages.
