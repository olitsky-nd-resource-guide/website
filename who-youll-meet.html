Who you’ll meet

Not sure who is on the other side of the door, call or email? Here’s who you’ll meet, so you know what to expect before you reach out.

People by name Who you meet at each office
People by name

The person you can ask for

  • Amy Tavares

    Program manager, Olitsky Career Readiness Program

    Career coaching on self-advocacy, disclosure and accommodations at work. Not sure where to start? Amy can point you the right way.

    See how to book with Amy Tavares
At each office

Who you meet when you book

Staff change, so these show the role you will meet, not one person.

  • Peer success coach

    Student Academic Success Center (SASC)

    Where you meet
    Posner Hall, 1st floor, or online
    What it’s like
    A trained peer coach. You talk about what’s hard, like planning or focus, and choose strategies to try.
    See Success Coaching

  • CaPS counselor

    Counseling and Psychological Services

    Where you meet
    Highmark Center, 4th floor, after you call to book
    What it’s like
    A first, private conversation about what is going on. Free, and you decide what to share.
    Call CaPS

  • Disability Resources staff

    Office of Disability Resources (ODR)

    Where you meet
    Online portal, then a meeting with ODR
    What it’s like
    You talk about how classes and exams go and what could help. You can start even without documentation.
    See the accommodations guide

  • College career consultant

    Career and Professional Development Center

    Where you meet
    Virtual or in person, booked in Handshake
    What it’s like
    The consultant for your college helps with your goals, like a job or internship search or your resume.
    See career consulting

  • HUB liaison

    The HUB

    Where you meet
    Warner Hall, lower level, or remote
    What it’s like
    Your liaison gives you personal help with questions about aid, billing, payments, registration or records.
    Find your HUB liaison

  • Housefellow

    Housing and Residential Education, if you live on campus

    Where you meet
    Your house community
    What it’s like
    Student Affairs staff who mentor students one-on-one, including help with everyday routines.
    Find your Housefellow
