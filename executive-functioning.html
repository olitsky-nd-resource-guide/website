Learn practical ways to plan, start and finish work, in a small group with a few other students.

How to start, step by step

Three steps. Everyone has a short call before joining.

  1. 1Step 1: Email the facilitator or call CaPS

    • your CMU email, or phone 412-268-2922
    • You need: your name and Andrew ID

    Say you want to join the Executive Functioning Skills Clinic. You can use the email below.

  2. 2Step 2: Have a short call first

    • Phone or Zoom with CaPS
    • You need: a few minutes

    CaPS calls this a pre-group consultation. It is a short chat to check the group fits what you need.

    Not sure it fits? That is what this call is for. You can ask about other options too.

  3. 3Step 3: Join the 3 sessions

    • Zoom
    • You need: the link CaPS sends you

    The group meets on Wednesdays from 1 to 2 pm. [CONFIRM SPRING DATES]

What to expect

Before

Have your Andrew ID and a rough idea of what gets in the way when you plan or start work.

During

A facilitator shares strategies and you try them with a few other students.

After

Keep using what works. Ask CaPS about other groups or one-on-one support if you want more.
