Sexual health care

Confidential care at University Health Services, from check-ups and birth control to STI testing and PrEP.

Official name: Sexual Health, University Health Services (UHS)

Call UHS: 412-268-2157

Select option 2 to schedule. Some appointments can also be booked online in HealthConnect.

Format
One-on-one
Where
Highmark Center, 100 Tech Street, Suite 308
Privacy
Confidential

What to expect

Before

Know what kind of appointment you want, like a check-up, birth control or STI testing.

During

Meet with a health care provider. UHS’s sexual health services are confidential.

After

Regular check-ups are part of taking care of your sexual health. Book your next one the same way.

How to start, step by step

Three steps. Book by phone, or online for some appointments.

  1. 1Step 1: Pick the care you need

    • University Health Services
    • You need: nothing

    Taking care of your sexual health includes personal hygiene and check-ups with a health care provider. UHS services include:

    • Gynecological exams
    • Pregnancy and birth control, including IUDs and Nexplanon
    • Testing and care for sexually transmitted infections (STIs)
    • Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP)
    • Support with unplanned pregnancies

  2. 2Step 2: Call or book online

    • Phone or HealthConnect
    • You need: nothing

    Call 412-268-2157 and select option 2. You can say: “Hi. I would like to schedule an appointment to get tested for sexually transmitted infections (STIs).”

    Some appointments can be scheduled online through HealthConnect (opens in a new tab). You can also send a question under Messages, or book a virtual visit with a clinician under Visits.

    Your care is confidential. UHS offers many confidential services to support your sexual health.

  3. 3Step 3: Go to your appointment

    • Highmark Center, 100 Tech Street, Suite 308
    • You need: your appointment time

    Go to University Health Services in the Highmark Center, 100 Tech Street, Suite 308.
