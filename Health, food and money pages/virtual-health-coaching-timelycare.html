TimelyCare health coaching

Free virtual coaching through TimelyCare on meal planning, food allergies, weight management and eating disorders.

Official name: Virtual Health Coaching, TimelyCare

Go to TimelyCare (opens in a new tab)

Free for enrolled students. Sign up with your andrew.cmu.edu email.

Format
One-on-one
Where
Virtual, TimelyCare app or website
Topics
Meal planning, allergies, weight, eating disorders

What to expect

Before

Think about what you’d like help with: meal planning, food allergies, weight management or eating disorders.

During

Meet virtually with a health coach for coaching and resources on your goal.

After

Keep using TimelyCare for more support, including TalkNow if you need to talk to someone 24/7.

Questions? Call University Health Services at 412-268-2157.

How to start, step by step

Three steps, all online.

  1. 1Step 1: Open TimelyCare

    • timelycare.com/CMU or the TimelyCare app
    • You need: a phone or computer

    Go to timelycare.com/CMU (opens in a new tab), or download the TimelyCare app from the App Store or Google Play.

  2. 2Step 2: Register with your Andrew email

    • TimelyCare sign-up page
    • You need: your andrew.cmu.edu email

    Create an account using your andrew.cmu.edu email address.

    Use your Andrew email. You must use your andrew.cmu.edu email address. A college email such as tepper.cmu.edu won’t work.

  3. 3Step 3: Follow the prompts to health coaching

    • TimelyCare app or website
    • You need: nothing

    Follow the guided prompts and choose health coaching. TimelyCare provides resources and coaching on meal planning, food allergies, weight management and eating disorders.
