CMU Pantry

The CMU Pantry is a free food resource for all undergraduate and graduate students. Shop once a week for fresh produce, pantry staples and hygiene items.

Official name: CMU Pantry

Go to the CMU Pantry page (opens in a new tab)

Free for all undergraduate and graduate students. No questions asked.

Format
On your own
Where
Residence on Fifth, 4700 Fifth Ave
How often
Once a week

What to expect

Before

Fill out the form and book a time. You’ll get an email confirming your day and time.

During

Pick up fresh fruits and vegetables (depending on the season), staples like pasta, rice, peanut butter and canned goods, plus toiletries and hygiene items.

After

Come back once a week. Fill out the form again before each visit.

How to start, step by step

Three steps: a form, a time slot, then shopping.

  1. 1Step 1: Fill out the Pantry Shopping Form

    • CMU Pantry website
    • You need: nothing

    Complete the “Pantry Shopping Form” on the CMU Pantry page (opens in a new tab). The first time, choose “This is my first time visiting the Pantry.”

    Fill it out every time. The form must be completed for every visit, not just the first one.

  2. 2Step 2: Pick a day and time

    • Pantry Shopping Time Calendar
    • You need: nothing

    After you submit the form, you’ll go to the Pantry Shopping Time Calendar to pick your day and time. You’ll get an email confirming it.

    Need to change it? Use the links in your confirmation email to reschedule or cancel.

  3. 3Step 3: Shop during your time slot

    • Residence on Fifth, 4700 Fifth Ave
    • You need: nothing

    Go to the Residence on Fifth Avenue (4700 Fifth Avenue). When you enter the building, turn left and walk to the dining room outside the Pantry.

    Be on time. If you arrive outside your reservation time, you may be asked to wait for space to open up before you can shop.
