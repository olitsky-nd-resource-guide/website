What to expect

Before

Think about the concern that led you to reach out, and anything that could help right now.

During

A Student Support Resources staff member reaches out to you to talk about what you need.

After

The team gives one-on-one support and works with campus and community resources to coordinate care.

How to start, step by step

Four steps, starting with one form or call.

  1. 1Step 1: Submit the form or call

    • Online or phone
    • You need: nothing

    Submit a CMU Cares Form (opens in a new tab) online, or call the Office of the Dean of Students at 412-268-2075.

  2. 2Step 2: Tell us about your concern

    • In the form or on the call
    • You need: a few words about your concern

    Tell the team about the concern that led you to reach out. It can be a wellbeing concern for yourself or someone else, a challenging situation, recovering from an injury, or needing to borrow a laptop while you save for a new one or wait for repairs. Not sure who to ask or what you need? The team can point you in the right direction.

  3. 3Step 3: Share what could help

    • In the form or on the call
    • You need: nothing

    Share anything you think could help right now.

  4. 4Step 4: Hear back from the team

    • Student Support Resources
    • You need: nothing

    A Student Support Resources staff member will reach out to you.
