About this guide

A plain-language guide to support at CMU, sorted by what you need instead of which office runs it.

Why we made it

CMU has a lot of support, but it is usually listed by office. To use it, you often need to know the service exists, which office runs it, what it actually does and who to contact. That is a lot to figure out when you are already stuck.

This guide starts from what is going on for you, shows a few clear options, and spells out the next step: where to go, what to bring and what to say.

Who made it

The guide was built in Fall 2026 by Carnegie Mellon students, as a course project with Professor Andrew Begel, in partnership with the Olitsky Family Foundation Career Readiness Program at the Career and Professional Development Center.

Where the information comes from

Details come from each office’s official pages and from the resource gallery the Olitsky Program put together with those offices. Hours and steps can change, so check the official page linked on each resource before you go.
