Why we made it

Finding help shouldn’t mean knowing where to look first

CMU has a lot of support, but it is spread across many offices and websites. When we talked with students, the hard part was rarely the help itself. It was knowing where to look and what to do next.

  • Start from what you need. Topics are named after problems, not offices.
  • Same layout every time. Every resource tells you how to book and what happens next.
  • No personal details needed. Nothing asks for a diagnosis or documents.
