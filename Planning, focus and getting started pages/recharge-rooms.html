Recharge Rooms

A space during career fairs and networking events where you can rest, relax and refresh. Use it as often as you need.

Official name: Recharge Rooms, Olitsky Family Foundation Career Readiness Program

Find the event in Handshake (opens in a new tab)

Fidget objects, coloring pages and ear plugs are there for you to use.

Format
On your own
Where
At career fairs and networking events
When
During the event, as often as you need

What to expect

Before

Find out where the Recharge Room is before you arrive, so you know where to go if you need a break.

During

Take a break in a reduced-sensory space. Use the fidget objects, coloring pages or ear plugs, or just sit and rest.

After

Head back into the event when you’re ready. Come back as often as you need.

How to start, step by step

Three steps. Find the room, then use it whenever you need a break.

  1. 1Step 1: Check the event’s Handshake page

    • Handshake
    • You need: your Andrew ID

    Look up the career fair or networking event in Handshake (opens in a new tab). The event page is one place to find when and where the Recharge Room is.

  2. 2Step 2: Find it at the event

    • At the career fair or event
    • You need: nothing

    Check the map of the event, or ask a Career and Professional Development Center staff member where the Recharge Room is.

  3. 3Step 3: Rest as long as you need

    • The Recharge Room
    • You need: nothing

    Rest, relax and refresh. Fidget objects, coloring pages and ear plugs are available. Sometimes there’s also a person you can talk to if you need it.

    Come back as often as you need. You can use the space more than once during an event.
