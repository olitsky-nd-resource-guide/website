The Center Mentorship

The Center builds a supportive community that includes everyone at CMU. Mentoring is at the heart of its programs, like the Origins Pre-Fall Initiative and Graduate Women’s Gatherings.

Official name: The Center’s Mentorship Program, Center for Student Diversity and Inclusion

Email The Center

Or call 412-268-2150.

Format
Mentoring
Where
Cohon University Center, Lower Level 75
Who
Everyone at CMU

What to expect

Before

The Center’s community includes everyone at CMU. Reach out by email to ask about mentoring.

During

Connect with mentors and other students through The Center’s programs.

After

Stay involved in programs that build awareness and community at CMU.

How to start, step by step

Three steps, starting with one email.

  1. 1Step 1: Email The Center

    • Email
    • You need: nothing

    Email csdi@andrew.cmu.edu to ask about mentoring. You can use the email below.

  2. 2Step 2: Follow The Center on Instagram

    • Instagram
    • You need: nothing

    Follow @thecentercmu (opens in a new tab) to see programs and events.

  3. 3Step 3: Join the mailing list

    • Online form
    • You need: your CMU email

    Sign up for The Center’s newsletter (opens in a new tab) to hear about mentoring programs and events.
