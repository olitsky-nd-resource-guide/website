Getting academic accommodations

Changes like extra exam time or a quieter room, so a disability doesn’t get in the way of your classes.

Official name: Academic accommodations, Office of Disability Resources (ODR)

Start the application

Opens the ODR online portal in a new tab. Sign in with your Andrew ID.

How to start, step by step

Five steps across the ODR portal, your email and one meeting.

  1. 1Step 1: Apply online

    • ODR online portal
    • You need: your Andrew ID

    Sign in and choose Start New Application. It asks a few questions about you and your classes.

    Start term means the term you want accommodations to start, not when you started at CMU.

    Open the application

  2. 2Step 2: Get a confirmation

    • your CMU email
    • You need: nothing

    ODR emails you to say they got your form. If you don’t see it in a few days, check spam, then email ODR.

  3. 3Step 3: Send your documentation

    • upload in the ODR portal
    • You need: documents about your disability

    A letter or report from a provider who knows you. The documentation guidelines say what it should include. Upload it in the same portal where you applied.

    Don’t have documents yet? Email ODR anyway. They can tell you your options.

  4. 4Step 4: Meet with ODR

    • Zoom or the ODR office
    • You need: to talk about what gets in your way

    A staff member talks with you about how classes and exams go and what changes could help. Then they decide on your accommodations.

    Request a meeting

  5. 5Step 5: Share your letter, every semester

    • ODR portal, after approval
    • You need: your class list

    Once approved, send your Summary of Accommodations Memorandum to your instructors from the portal at the start of each semester.

    Can’t find the memo? It only shows up in the portal after you’re approved.

What to expect

Before

Have your Andrew ID. If you already have documents from a provider, keep them handy.

During

You talk with ODR about how classes and exams go and what could help.

After

Once approved, send your letter to instructors at the start of every semester.
