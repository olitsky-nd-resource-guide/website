Supplemental Instruction

Join weekly peer-led study sessions to practice course material, ask questions, and study with other students.

Official name: Supplemental Instruction (SI) Supported Courses, Student Academic Success Center (SASC)

View SI schedule

Opens the SASC Supplemental Instruction page in a new tab.

Format
Group
Where
In person or virtual, by course
How long
Varies by course

What to expect

Before

Check the session schedule and bring any notes, assignments, or questions from class.

During

Work through course material with other students and a peer SI leader.

After

Use what you practiced in class and return to future sessions whenever you need more support.

How to start, step by step

Three steps, from finding your course to joining a session.

  1. 1Step 1: Check which courses offer SI

    • SI website
    • You need: your course schedule

    Visit the official Supplemental Instruction website to see which courses have SI sessions this semester.

    Who it’s for: Students enrolled in courses offering SI this semester. These may include biology, chemistry, math, physics, engineering, and 15-122.

  2. 2Step 2: Find a session that works for you

    • SI session schedule
    • You need: your weekly availability

    Look at the available meeting times and locations for your course. Choose a session that fits your schedule.

    Where sessions meet: It varies by course. Most are in Posner Hall, with others in HOA 107, WEH 5302, Margaret Morrison A14, or on Zoom.

  3. 3Step 3: Attend a study session

    • Assigned classroom or Zoom
    • You need: your course materials

    Go to the listed classroom or join online if the session is virtual. Practice course material with other students and an SI leader.

    No sign-up needed. You don’t need to sign up ahead of time. Just sign in when you get there.
