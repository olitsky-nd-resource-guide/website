Academic accommodations

Changes like extra exam time or a quieter room, so a disability doesn’t get in the way of your classes.

Official name: Academic accommodations, Office of Disability Resources (ODR)

Start the application

Opens the ODR online portal in a new tab. Sign in with your Andrew ID.

Format
Step-by-step guide
Where
Online portal and ODR meeting
Timing
Apply at any time

What to expect

Before

Have your Andrew ID. If you already have documents from a provider, keep them handy.

During

You talk with ODR about how classes and exams go and what could help.

After

Once approved, send your letter to instructors at the start of every semester.

How to start, step by step

Six steps. Apply at any time. You can start even if you don’t have documentation yet.

  1. 1Step 1: Log into the ODR portal

    • ODR online portal
    • You need: your Andrew ID

    Log into the Disability Resources portal with your Andrew ID.

    Open the portal

  2. 2Step 2: Fill out the application

    • ODR online portal
    • You need: your Andrew ID

    Fill out the application for services. It asks a few questions about you and your classes.

    Start term means the term you want accommodations to start, not when you started at CMU.

  3. 3Step 3: Share documentation if you have it

    • upload in the ODR portal
    • You need: documentation from a licensed provider, if you have it

    A letter or report from a provider who knows you. The documentation guidelines say what it should include. Upload it in the same portal where you applied.

    Don’t have documentation yet? You can still start the process.

    Read the documentation guidelines

  4. 4Step 4: Meet with ODR

    • Zoom or the ODR office
    • You need: to talk about what gets in your way

    A staff member talks with you about what’s getting in the way in your classes and what could help. Then they decide on your accommodations.

  5. 5Step 5: Send your memo, every semester

    • ODR portal, after approval
    • You need: your class list

    If you’re approved, send your instructors your Summary of Accommodations Memorandum through the portal at the start of each semester.

    Can’t find the memo? It only shows up in the portal after you’re approved.

  6. 6Step 6: Meet briefly with each instructor

    • with each instructor
    • You need: your approved accommodations

    Meet briefly with each faculty member to talk about how your accommodations will work in their course. You don’t have to explain your needs from scratch.
