Support groups and connection hours

Small groups and skills clinics run by CaPS. Several could help neurodivergent students, including the Executive Functioning Skills Clinic.

Official name: Groups and Workshops, Counseling and Psychological Services (CaPS)

See current groups (opens in a new tab)

Or call CaPS at 412-268-2922 and ask about groups.

Format
Group
Where
Virtual
When
Wednesdays, 1 to 2 pm (Fall 2026)

What to expect

Before

A short pre-group consultation to talk about what you need and whether the group is a good fit.

During

Meet online with a facilitator and a few other students for 3 one-hour sessions.

After

Keep using what works. Ask CaPS about the next round, other groups or one-on-one support.

Dates change each semester. Check the current schedule (opens in a new tab) for upcoming rounds.

How to start, step by step

Three steps. Everyone has a short pre-group consultation before joining.

  1. 1Step 1: Review the groups

    • CaPS website
    • You need: nothing

    Look through the CaPS groups and workshops page (opens in a new tab) and pick one that fits.

  2. 2Step 2: Schedule a pre-group consultation

    • Email or phone
    • You need: your name and Andrew ID

    Before the round starts, call CaPS at 412-268-2922 or email the facilitator, Erin Unkefer, at eunkefer@andrew.cmu.edu. You can use the email below.

    Reach out early. The consultation needs to happen before the round’s first session.

  3. 3Step 3: Attend all 3 sessions

    • Virtual
    • You need: the link CaPS sends you

    Groups run in rounds of 3 sessions, Wednesdays 1 to 2 pm. Round 2 for Fall 2026 meets Nov 4, 11 and 18.
