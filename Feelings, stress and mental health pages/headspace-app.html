Headspace

CMU students get full access to Headspace when they sign up with their Andrew email. It has Meditation, Sleep Support, Movement and Focus content. CMU also offers academic courses on mindfulness and meditation.

Official name: Headspace and Mindfulness Courses, CMU Wellbeing

Sign up for Headspace (opens in a new tab)

Free when you sign up with your @andrew.cmu.edu email.

Format
On your own
Where
Headspace: online. Courses: check the course listing
How long
Start with the 10-day Basics course

What to expect

Before

You don’t need any experience with meditation. Just your Andrew email.

During

Short guided sessions for stress, focus and sleep that you can do on your own time.

After

Keep going with what helps. If you want to go deeper, CMU also offers mindfulness courses for credit.

How to start, step by step

Three steps, all on your phone or computer.

  1. 1Step 1: Subscribe with your Andrew email

    • CMU Headspace sign-up page
    • You need: your @andrew.cmu.edu email

    Enroll at work.headspace.com/cmu (opens in a new tab) using your @andrew.cmu.edu email address.

    Use your Andrew email. That’s how you get the full content for free through CMU’s subscription.

  2. 2Step 2: Open Headspace

    • Headspace app or website
    • You need: the account you just made

    Log in on your phone or computer with the same account.

  3. 3Step 3: Start the Basics course

    • Anywhere
    • You need: a few quiet minutes a day

    “Basics” is a 10-day beginner’s course and a great place to start. You can also try a one-minute meditation before an exam, or search for the emotion you’re feeling.

Want a class instead?

See courses (opens in a new tab)

CMU has academic courses on mindfulness and meditation. Look at the Wellbeing courses page (opens in a new tab), then talk with your academic advisor. You can say:

I’m interested in the Mindful Living course. Can you help me see where it fits in my schedule?
