Counseling at CaPS

A free, private appointment to talk about what’s going on. Together you decide what kind of support fits next.

Official name: Initial Consultation, Counseling and Psychological Services (CaPS)

Call CaPS: 412-268-2922

Free and confidential for students at the Pittsburgh campus. Need help today? Walk in or call for a same-day visit.

Format
One-on-one
Where
Highmark Center or Morewood Gardens
How long
45 to 50 minutes

What to expect

Before

You don’t need to prepare. If it helps, jot down a few words about what’s been hard.

During

Talk freely and privately about what’s going on. The counselor listens and asks questions.

After

You decide next steps together: short-term therapy at CaPS (most students use 6 to 8 sessions) or a referral to care in the community.

Need help today? CaPS has same-day visits for urgent needs. Walk in or call 412-268-2922.

How to start, step by step

Four steps, starting with one phone call.

  1. 1Step 1: Call and ask for a consultation

    • Phone, CaPS main line
    • You need: your name

    Call 412-268-2922 and ask to schedule a consultation. You can say: “Hi, my name is [YOUR NAME] and I’d like to schedule a consultation for individual therapy.”

    Don’t know what to ask for? “A consultation” is enough. You don’t have to explain everything on the phone.

  2. 2Step 2: Set a reminder

    • Your calendar and CMU email
    • You need: the time they give you

    Add the appointment to your calendar. CaPS also sends an automatic reminder email the business day before.

  3. 3Step 3: Read the FAQ

    • CaPS website
    • You need: nothing

    The CaPS FAQ page (opens in a new tab) answers common questions about privacy, cost and what happens after the first meeting.

  4. 4Step 4: Go to your appointment

    • Highmark Center, 4th floor, or Morewood Gardens, 2nd floor
    • You need: nothing

    Go to the location they gave you when you booked: Highmark Center, 4th floor (100 Tech St), or Morewood Gardens, 2nd floor (1060 Morewood Ave). The meeting lasts 45 to 50 minutes.
