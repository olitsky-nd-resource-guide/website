Privacy and accessibility

What this guide keeps, what it never asks for, and how CMU’s privacy notice fits in.

The short version

Four things to know

  • No account needed

    You can browse, search, save resources and use Help me choose without signing in.

  • We never ask about a diagnosis

    Nothing here asks for medical details, documents or which services you already use.

  • Saved resources stay on this device

    My Resources is kept in your browser, not sent to us. Clearing this site’s data in your browser clears it.

  • Other sites have their own rules

    Booking tools like WCOnline or Handshake follow their own privacy policies once you leave this guide.

This guide

What happens when you use it

What we collect
Nothing. The guide has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.
Saved resources
Stored in this browser on this device. We can’t see them. Un-save a resource, or clear this site’s data in your browser, to remove them.
Search and Help me choose
Your Help me choose answers stay in this browser tab and are gone when you close it. What you search for becomes part of the page address. Like any web address, it is sent to the site’s host when the page loads and shows up in your browser history.
Hosting and fonts
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and your browser also loads fonts from Google Fonts and code from jsDelivr and jQuery. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser details. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
When you email us
Email links open your own email app. Nothing is sent until you send it.
Cookies
The guide doesn’t set any cookies.
CMU’s privacy notice

The official notice, summarized

Carnegie Mellon’s Privacy Notice covers CMU websites and services. Open any part for a one-line summary.

01What CMU collects and why

Things like contact details, browsing activity on CMU sites and education records, used to provide its services, run its programs, improve its websites and keep them secure.

02How long CMU keeps it

Only as long as it needs to for the reason it was collected, or longer when the law or a dispute requires it.

03The reasons CMU is allowed to use it

To carry out an agreement with you, meet its legal obligations, pursue its legitimate interests, or because you gave consent.

04Who CMU shares it with

Staff and contractors who need it, service providers working for CMU, anyone you authorize, and anyone the law requires. CMU doesn’t sell or rent it.

05Cookies and similar tools

CMU sites use cookies, including Google Analytics, to work properly, remember your choices, see how the sites are used and show you relevant CMU information. You can block cookies in your browser, but some pages may not work.

06Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to ask CMU what it has about you, or ask it to correct or delete that information, by emailing privacy@andrew.cmu.edu.

07Links to other sites

CMU isn’t responsible for the privacy practices of other sites, so check their policies.

08Social networks and public forums

What you share on social sites or public forums can be collected and used by others, and those sites follow their own privacy policies.

09Stopping marketing emails

Use the unsubscribe link in the email, email it-help@cmu.edu, or reply STOP to a text. You will still get important messages about your accounts.

10Data sent to other countries

CMU and its providers may process your information outside your country, with safeguards to keep it protected.

11How CMU keeps data safe

Security policies, controls and staff training. CMU will tell you about a breach that affects your information as the law requires.

12Children’s data

CMU services meant for children under 13 need a parent’s verified consent first.

13Changes to the notice

CMU posts changes on the notice page and gives extra notice for changes that materially affect your rights.

14How to contact CMU

Email privacy@andrew.cmu.edu, or write to the Data Protection Officer at 5000 Forbes Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15213.
