Privacy and accessibility

No accounts, no tracking. Here is exactly what the guide keeps and where.

What this guide keeps

  • Saved resources are stored in this browser on this device. We can’t see them. Un-save a resource, or clear this site’s data in your browser, to remove them.
  • Help me choose answers stay in this browser tab and are gone when you close it.
  • What you search for becomes part of the page address. Like any web address, it is sent to the site’s host when the page loads and shows up in your browser history.

The guide has no sign-in and no analytics or advertising cookies.

Services the guide uses

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To show you a page, your browser also loads fonts from Google Fonts and code from jsDelivr and jQuery. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser details when they send files. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how GitHub handles this.

Links to CMU offices and other sites take you to pages with their own privacy policies.

Carnegie Mellon’s privacy notice

Carnegie Mellon’s Privacy Notice explains how the university collects and uses personal information.

Accessibility

We built the guide to work with a keyboard and screen readers, with plain language and a few clear choices on each page. If something gets in your way, tell us or report a digital accessibility barrier to CMU.
